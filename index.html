Mokoji — Store, Repairs & Back Office

A steam-iron store and repair service in one Flutter app, sitting on top of a complete back office. Customers shop and request repairs, technicians handle jobs and sell from their own stock, and the admin runs inventory, sales, cash and reports — three roles, one database.

  • Every permission enforced in PostgreSQL via RLS — even direct API calls see only what the role allows
  • Sensitive operations (sales, stock issues, repairs) run only through atomic SECURITY DEFINER RPCs
  • Append-only ledgers for cash and stock, price snapshots on every sale, private photos via signed URLs
  • Cart & checkout, wallet, InstaPay and refunds; live repair queue; FCM push through an Edge Function

Al-Fardos Management — Accounting & ERP

Arabic accounting and business management for small and medium businesses: sales and purchase invoices, customers and suppliers, cashboxes, expenses, stock and reports — all posted through one double-entry ledger so the numbers always add up.

  • Every operation is one balanced posting written in a single atomic Firestore transaction — balances, stock, stats and audit trail together, or nothing
  • Idempotent posting (a double tap or retry never posts twice); money stored as integer minor units
  • Security rules forbid deletes and unbalanced writes — covered by emulator tests; pure-Dart accounting core fully unit-tested
  • Custom roles & granular permissions, audit log, Arabic PDF invoices, Excel/CSV export, manufactured products from recipes

Length Factory — Order & Inventory System

A second, independently-architected factory platform — built to prove the first wasn't a fluke. Admins run products, orders, customers and payments; customers order from a live catalog; workers see only the production queue, never a price.
